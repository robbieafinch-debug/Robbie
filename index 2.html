01

What this is

Pacific Voice supports responsible investment in the Pacific by giving communities a way to raise concerns about unethical activity.

Most foreign investment in the Pacific is done fairly. It brings jobs, infrastructure, and growth, and it is welcome. Yet unethical activity can cause real harm. When a community, a landowner group, or a government is taken advantage of — through a mining accident, an oil spill, illegal logging, or a one-sided commercial agreement — the people affected often have no clear way to seek justice. Cases that should reach the courts are lost because the people affected don't know where to start, who to trust, or how to fund a claim.

Pacific Voice changes that. Concerns submitted here are reviewed by qualified lawyers from Pacific Legal Network — a 25-year-old network of legal firms across 21 Pacific countries. Cases that can be progressed will be, with the support of litigation funders where appropriate. Holding unethical actors to account protects everyone: communities, governments, and the responsible companies who do things the right way.

02

How it works

  1. 1

    You submit your concern

    Tell us what happened, who was affected, and what evidence exists. Submit through this site, or ask a local PLN lawyer to do it on your behalf. Submitting commits you to nothing.

  2. 2

    A lawyer in your country reviews it

    Your submission is routed to a PLN lawyer in your jurisdiction. They will review it within 30 days and contact you to discuss what they have found.

  3. 3

    We respond with a path forward

    If your concern looks like a viable legal claim, we will discuss how to take it forward, including litigation funding where appropriate. If it isn't, we will tell you why and point you to other organisations that may be able to help.

03

Who Pacific Voice is for

Communities and landowners

If your community or land has been harmed by unethical activity — an extraction project, shipping, infrastructure, or fishing done without proper care or consent — Pacific Voice is a way to have your situation reviewed by a lawyer for free.

Local governments and authorities

If your government, ministry, or authority is dealing with the consequences of unethical commercial conduct in your country, Pacific Voice can help you understand your legal options.

Civil society organisations

NGOs, churches and community groups can submit concerns on behalf of the communities they support, with the community's consent.

What this isn't for

Pacific Voice is for raising unethical activity that harms communities or the environment. It is not a complaint line about investment in general, and we welcome responsible investment in the Pacific. We do not handle individual employment disputes, family law, criminal matters, or ordinary commercial disputes that don't involve community or environmental harm. If your concern doesn't fit here, we will point you in the right direction.

Have a concern to raise?

Submitting takes about ten minutes. Your information stays with us and the assessing lawyer.
